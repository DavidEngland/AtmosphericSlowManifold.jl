`AtmosphericSlowManifold.jl` (ASM.jl) provides a data-driven, mathematically rigorous alternative to classical atmospheric boundary layer (ABL) turbulence closures by unifying Geometric Singular Perturbation Theory (GSPT) with Weak-form Sparse Identification of Non-linear Dynamics (WSINDy).

---

### Core Architectural & Mathematical Alignment

The framework's primary innovation is replacing empirical thresholds like the critical Richardson number ($Ri_{\text{cr}} \approx 0.2 - 0.25$) with geometric indicators of normal hyperbolicity loss.

```
       [ Observational Data Ingestion ] (CASES-99, FLOSS, BLLAST, SHEBA)
                      │
                      ▼
       [ Intrinsic Coordinate Mapping ] ──> z = (η₁, η₂, η₃, R, Ω, χ, Π_G, λ_min)
                      │
                      ▼
       [ Fast-Slow GSPT Decomposition ] ──> Fast TKE modes (y), Slow Mean Profiles (x)
                      │                    Fold Locus: det(D_y g(z)) = 0
                      ▼
       [ Weak-Form WSINDy Engine ]      ──> Space-Time Projections onto Test Functions
                      │                    Hard Constraints: Positivity (K_m ≥ 0),
                      ▼                                    Monotonic Energy Dissipation
       [ Model Selection & Pareto ]     ──> AIC / BIC Metrics & Pareto Front Extraction
                      │
                      ▼
       [ Calibration & Uncertainty ]    ──> Hierarchical Turing & 95% Credibility Ribbons
                      │
                      ▼
       [ Automated Manuscript Exports ] ──> Publication LaTeX Tables & Vector Figures

```

| **Hard Physical Constraints** | `src/Discovery/ConstraintBuilder.jl`<br>

### Executable Workflow Strategy for `make manuscript`

To fully automate paper artifact generation directly from raw data artifacts, the executable pipeline in `scripts/` is organized into three sequential stages:

```
                          make manuscript
                                 │
         ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
         ▼                       ▼                       ▼
 scripts/run_pde_closure    scripts/run_campaign     scripts/generate_paper
    _benchmark.jl               _exports.jl               _figures.jl
         │                       │                       │
         ▼                       ▼                       ▼
  • AIC/BIC metrics       • MCMC/VI posteriors    • High-impact figures
  • Pareto curves         • 95% $K_m(z)$ ribbons  • Compiled .tex tables
  • LaTeX term tables     • Multi-site summary    • Complete manuscript
    (discovered eqns)       tables                  ZIP bundle

```

1. **`scripts/run_pde_closure_benchmark.jl`:** Synthesizes canonical ABL profiles, runs WSINDy candidate scans across noise levels, evaluates AIC/BIC, extracts Pareto fronts, and dumps LaTeX term tables into `reports/generated/pde_benchmark/tables/`.
2. **`scripts/run_campaign_exports.jl`:** Loads observational campaign data across sites, executes point/sample calibration, evaluates $K_m(z)$ quantile ribbons, and writes cross-site summary tables to `reports/generated/campaign_exports/tables/`.
3. **`scripts/generate_paper_figures.jl`:** Orchestrates benchmark and campaign runs, compiling vector graphic plots (`.pdf`/`.svg`) and standalone `.tex` tables into a unified publication bundle under `reports/manuscript/`.
